Meridian

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · Last updated 28 October 2026

Meridian is a personal tool that its owner runs on their own computer to make short clips and post them to their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. This policy explains what information Meridian gets when an account is connected, where it's kept, and how to remove it.

  1. Who this covers

    Meridian has one user: its owner. It only ever connects to the owner's own accounts. It has no public sign-up and doesn't collect information about anyone else.

  2. What Meridian receives

    When an account is connected through a platform's official sign-in, Meridian receives only what that platform shares for the permissions you approve:

    InformationWhy Meridian needs it
    Access and refresh tokensTo upload a video you choose to post, without signing in each time
    Account ID (for TikTok, the open ID)To know which account a token belongs to
    Display name or usernameTo show which account is connected
    Upload results (post ID, link, status)To show whether a post worked and link to it
    Public view, like and comment counts of posted clips (YouTube)To see how posted clips perform

    Meridian doesn't ask for or receive your password, your messages, your followers, your contacts, or your location.

  3. How it's used

    • Videos are uploaded only when you press a button to post a specific video.
    • Information is used only to run Meridian's own features: showing the connected account, posting, and tracking how your posts do.
    • Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or training any model.

  4. Where it's stored

    Everything stays on the owner's computer, in Meridian's local data folder. Meridian has no server of its own and no cloud database. Sign-in tokens are kept by the local helper program and are never shown in Meridian's web page.

  5. Sharing

    Meridian doesn't sell, rent or share your information with anyone. It sends information only to the platform it came from (for example, a TikTok token goes only to TikTok) in order to post the videos you choose.

  6. How long it's kept

    A token is kept until you disconnect the account, or until it expires and can't be refreshed. Post records are kept until you delete the clip in Meridian.

  7. Removing your information

    Press Unlink next to the account in Meridian, which deletes its stored token right away. You can also remove Meridian's access in the platform's own settings. On TikTok, that's Settings and privacy, then Security, then Manage app permissions.

  8. Children

    Meridian isn't meant for children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

  9. Changes to this policy

    This policy may be updated if Meridian changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

Questions about this policy: contact the owner of the Meridian clips channel through its YouTube or TikTok profile.
